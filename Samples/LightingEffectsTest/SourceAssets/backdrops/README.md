## Exact imagegen prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: opaque ground-only background for a top-down 2D game room, landscape 4:3 composition, intended display 800 x 600. Primary request: a flat snow-covered grassy ground surface filling the entire image, viewed straight down orthographically. Thin uneven white snow blankets most of short muted green grass, with scattered exposed grass patches showing through and subtle pale blue-grey surface texture. Match a simple lightly painted game ground texture, evenly detailed throughout, moderate detail, no dominant focal point. Neutral diffuse daylight, restrained contrast so snow particles, rain and lighting can be compared over it. Constraints: ground surface only, edge-to-edge texture, no sky, horizon, trees, rocks, buildings, paths, characters, footprints, falling snow, rain, fog, cloud shadows, text, borders, watermark, baked directional light or cast shadows. It must be a static snowy ground backdrop, not a picture of a weather effect.
